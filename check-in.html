Before you fly

Make the airport part feel easy.

Three small checks can turn a rushed departure into a calm one. Keep this page handy before travel day.

01

Check in online

Most airlines open online check-in 24 hours before departure. Use the airline link in your booking confirmation and save your boarding pass to your phone.

02

Review your documents

Check that the passenger names match your passport, your passport is valid for the destination, and any visa or entry requirements are covered.

03

Plan your arrival

Leave time for traffic, bag drop, security, and the walk to your gate. Your flight card shows the terminal and gate when those details are available.

+

Quick reminder

Airlines can change gates and schedules. Check your flight status again before leaving for the airport.
