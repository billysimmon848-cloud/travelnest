Good to know

Questions, answered.

The useful stuff, without the fine-print maze.

How do I check my flight status?

Enter the booking reference your advisor sent you on the homepage. We will show your latest route, timing, gate, and journey progress.

Can I book flights with multiple airlines?

Yes. We can coordinate journeys across different airlines and keep the itinerary details together for you.

What happens if my plans change?

Contact your travel advisor before making changes. We will explain the fare rules and available options for your booking.

Can you help with special travel needs?

Tell us what you need as early as possible and we will coordinate the request with the airline.

When should I arrive at the airport?

For international flights, plan to arrive around three hours before departure. Your airline or advisor may recommend more time for busy travel periods.
