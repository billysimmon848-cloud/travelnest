TRAVEL FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers to common flight tracking and travel questions.

How do I track my flight?

Enter your flight tracking number into the tracking field at the top of this page and select Track Flight.

What information can I see?

Depending on the flight profile, available information may include the passenger name, airline, flight number, route, travel date, departure time, flight duration, terminal, seat, class, booking reference and current status.

Why does my flight show an error?

If the flight has been placed in an Error status, an update from the flight controller may be displayed on the tracking page.

Can I track a flight without an account?

Public flight tracking can be accessed using the tracking number provided for the flight. No controller account is required to view the public tracking result.

Why can't I find my flight?

Make sure the tracking number is entered correctly. If the number is still not found, contact the appropriate travel support team.

Does the tracking page show a live aircraft map?

This tracking service displays the journey information available for the flight profile. Live aircraft positioning is not shown unless a real flight-location data source has been connected.
